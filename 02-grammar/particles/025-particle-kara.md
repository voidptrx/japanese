# Lesson 025 - Particle から

## 1. What I learned

- I learned how `から` marks a starting point.
- I can use `から` for the starting place of movement.
- I can use `から` for a starting time.
- I can distinguish a time marked by `に` from a starting time marked by `から`.
- I can attach `から` to a complete noun phrase.
- I can combine `から` with particles learned in previous lessons.
- I understand that the verb and context affect the translation.

## 2. What is the Particle から?

In the uses studied in this lesson, `から` marks a starting point.

- Place + `から`: from that place
- Time + `から`: from that time, starting at that time

The place or time before `から` identifies the starting point.

## 3. Cure Dolly's Explanation

Cure Dolly introduces a basic spatial use of `から` in Chapter 16,
in the section "The most basic use of the から particle".

The structural idea is "from": the noun phrase before `から` identifies the point of origin.

In this lesson, we apply the idea of a starting point to places and times.

Other uses of `から` will be studied separately.

## 4. から - A Starting Place

Example:

うちからがっこうにいきます。= I go from home to school.

- うちから: starting place, from home
- がっこうに: destination, to school
- いきます: predicate, go

`から` answers "From where?" in this example.

Example:

がっこうからうちへかえります。= I return home from school.

School is the starting place. Home is the direction of the movement.

## 5. Starting Place, Destination, Direction and Action Location

| Phrase       | Meaning       | Role                    |
| ------------ | ------------- | ----------------------- |
| がっこうから | from school   | starting place          |
| がっこうに   | to school     | destination of movement |
| がっこうへ   | toward school | direction of movement   |
| がっこうで   | at school     | location of an action   |

Compare:

がっこうでほんをよみます。= I read a book at school.\
がっこうからうちにかえります。= I return home from school.

`で` marks where the reading happens.
`から` marks where the return journey starts.

With the movement verbs studied here, `に` and `へ` can often be used
with the same destination, with a difference in focus.

## 6. から - A Starting Time

Example:

はちじからほんをよみます。= I read a book starting at eight o'clock.

はちじから identifies the starting time of the reading.

When describing a future plan, the sentence can be translated as:\
"I will read a book starting at eight o'clock."

The sentence does not specify an ending time.

## 7. Time に and Time から

| Sentence                   | Meaning                                  |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| はちじにほんをよみます。   | I read a book at eight o'clock.          |
| はちじからほんをよみます。 | I read a book starting at eight o'clock. |

In the first sentence, `に` marks the time associated with the reading.
In the second sentence, `から` explicitly presents eight o'clock as the starting time.

Keep the role of the time phrase separate from the verb's meaning and the context.

## 8. から with Noun Phrases

Example:

[わたしのせんせいのへや] からこうえんへいきます。= I go to the park from my teacher's room.

The complete phrase naming the starting place is わたしのせんせいのへや.
Its head noun is へや. `の` expresses relationships inside the noun phrase.
`から` follows the complete phrase and marks it as the starting place.

The brackets are analysis aids. They are not required Japanese punctuation.

## 9. から with Other Particles

Example:

わたしのともだちとでんしゃでうちからがっこうにいきます。=
I go from home to school by train with my friend.

| Part               | Role                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- |
| わたしのともだちと | companion: with my friend |
| でんしゃで         | transportation: by train  |
| うちから           | starting place: from home |
| がっこうに         | destination: to school    |
| いきます           | predicate: go             |

Each particle keeps its own role.

がっこうへ can also be used here to present school as the direction of movement.

## 10. Sentence Analysis

Context: the sentence describes tomorrow's plan.

せんせいもはちじからへやでほんをよみます。=
The teacher will also read a book in the room starting at eight o'clock.

| Part       | Role                              |
| ---------- | --------------------------------- |
| せんせいも | additional person: the teacher    |
| はちじから | starting time: from eight o'clock |
| へやで     | location of the reading action    |
| ほんを     | object: a book                    |
| よみます   | predicate: read                   |

`も` adds せんせい as another person to whom the statement applies.
`から` marks the starting time. `で` marks the action location.
The future interpretation comes from the plan context.

## 11. Important Distinctions

### A starting time and continuation until now

`から` does not by itself say that an activity began in the past
and has continued until now.

In a plan, はちじからほんをよみます can mean
"I will read a book starting at eight o'clock."

Whether "since" is an appropriate translation depends on the verb form
and the context.

Forms for describing an ongoing action will be studied separately.

### An omitted subject and context

はちじからへやでほんをよみます。=
I will read a book in the room starting at eight o'clock.

Here, the exercise context is that the speaker is describing their own
plan for tomorrow.

The Japanese subject is omitted. "I" is supplied by that context.

## 12. Important Concepts

- `から` can mark a starting place or a starting time.
- The phrase before `から` identifies the starting point.
- `に` can mark a destination or a time associated with an event.
- `へ` can mark the direction of movement.
- `で` can mark the location of an action or a means of transportation.
- `から` can follow a complete noun phrase.
- The verb and context determine how the whole sentence is understood.
- `から` alone does not establish continuation from the past until now.
- Other uses of `から` will be studied separately.
