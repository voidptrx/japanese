# Lesson 026 - Particle まで

## 1. What I learned

- I learned how `まで` marks an endpoint or limit.
- I can use `まで` for the endpoint of movement.
- I can use `まで` for an ending time.
- I can combine `から` and `まで` to describe a range.
- I can distinguish a time point from a starting time and an ending time.
- I can attach `まで` to a complete noun phrase.
- I can combine `まで` with particles learned in previous lessons.

## 2. What is the Particle まで?

In the uses studied in this lesson, `まで` marks an endpoint or limit.

- Place + `まで`: to that place, as far as that place
- Time + `まで`: until that time

The phrase before `まで` identifies the endpoint.

## 4. まで - An Endpoint of Movement

Example:

がっこうまでいきます。= I go as far as the school.

がっこうまで identifies the endpoint of the movement described here.
`まで` presents the extent of the journey.

## 5. から and まで with Places

Example:

うちからがっこうまでいきます。= I go from home to school.

| Part         | Role                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------- |
| うちから     | starting place: from home |
| がっこうまで | endpoint: to school       |
| いきます     | predicate: go             |

`から` marks the beginning of the route.
`まで` marks its endpoint.

## 6. まで - An Ending Time

New time word:

くじ = nine o'clock

The optional kanji spelling is 九時.

Example:

くじまでほんをよみます。= I read a book until nine o'clock.

くじまで identifies the ending time of the reading.
The sentence does not specify when the reading starts.

`まで` can be used without an explicit から phrase.

## 7. から and まで with Times

Example:

はちじからくじまでほんをよみます。= I read a book from eight to nine o'clock.

| Part       | Role                              |
| ---------- | --------------------------------- |
| はちじから | starting time: from eight o'clock |
| くじまで   | ending time: until nine o'clock   |
| ほんを     | object: a book                    |
| よみます   | predicate: read                   |

`から` and `まで` together define the time range.

When describing my plan for tomorrow, the sentence can mean:
"I will read a book from eight to nine o'clock."

## 8. まで with Noun Phrases

Example:

[わたしのせんせいのへや] までいきます。= I go as far as my teacher's room.

The complete phrase naming the endpoint is わたしのせんせいのへや.
Its head noun is へや.
`の` expresses relationships inside the noun phrase.
`まで` follows the complete phrase and marks the endpoint.

The brackets are analysis aids. They are not required Japanese punctuation.

## 9. Time に, から and まで

| Phrase   | Meaning                             | Role          |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------- |
| くじに   | at nine o'clock                     | time point    |
| くじから | from nine o'clock, starting at nine | starting time |
| くじまで | until nine o'clock                  | ending time   |

Compare:

くじにほんをよみます。= I read a book at nine o'clock.\
くじからほんをよみます。= I read a book starting at nine o'clock.\
くじまでほんをよみます。= I read a book until nine o'clock.

The particle determines how nine o'clock relates to the reading.

## 10. Sentence Analysis

Example:

はちじにともだちとバスでうちからがっこうまでいきます。=
At eight o'clock, I go from home to school by bus with my friend.

| Part         | Role                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| はちじに     | time of the journey: at eight o'clock |
| ともだちと   | companion: with my friend             |
| バスで       | transportation: by bus                |
| うちから     | starting place: from home             |
| がっこうまで | endpoint: to school                   |
| いきます     | predicate: go                         |

Each particle keeps its own role.

`に` marks the time here.
`から` and `まで` mark the two ends of the route.

The subject is omitted. "I" is supplied by the exercise context.

## 11. Important Distinctions

### A destination and an endpoint

がっこうにいきます。= I go to school.

`に` marks school as the destination.

がっこうまでいきます。= I go as far as the school.

`まで` highlights the endpoint or extent of the movement described.

### から and translation context

The time phrase くじから marks a starting point.
A neutral translation is "from nine o'clock" or "starting at nine o'clock".
"Since" can be appropriate with a suitable verb form and context.

`から` alone does not establish that an activity began in the past and has continued until now.

## 12. Important Concepts

- `まで` marks an endpoint or limit in the patterns studied here.
- With a place, it can mean "to" or "as far as".
- With a time, it can mean "until".
- `から` marks a starting point.
- `から` and `まで` can describe a spatial or temporal range.
- `まで` can be used without stating the starting point.
- `まで` can follow a complete noun phrase.
- Other particles retain their own roles.
- The verb and context affect the translation.
- Other uses of `まで` will be studied separately.
