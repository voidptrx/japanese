# Lesson 037 - Question Word: いくら

## 1. What Is いくら?

いくら means "how much".
It can ask about amounts, but its most common beginner-level use is asking about prices.

**Example:**

この本はいくらですか。= How much is this book?

## 2. Asking About Prices

Basic pattern:

[Topic] は いくらですか。

**Example:**

その本はいくらですか。= How much is that book?

- その本は: Topic
- いくらです: Predicate asking for the price
- か: Question particle

いくらですか uses the familiar noun-predicate question structure.

## 3. Answering Price Questions

円（えん）means Japanese yen (¥).

| Japanese | Reading      | Price  |
| -------- | ------------ | ------ |
| 百円     | ひゃくえん   | ¥100   |
| 五百円   | ごひゃくえん | ¥500   |
| 千円     | せんえん     | ¥1,000 |

**Example:**

この本はいくらですか。= How much is this book?\
五百円です。= It is 500 yen.\
この本は五百円です。= This book is 500 yen.

**Example:**

この本は千円ではありません。= This book is not 1,000 yen.

## 4. いくら vs. Other Question Words

| Question               | Meaning                | Information |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------- |
| これは何ですか。       | What is this?          | Identity    |
| これは誰の本ですか。   | Whose book is this?    | Ownership   |
| この本はいくらですか。 | How much is this book? | Price       |

Different question words request different information.

## 5. Combining Familiar Structures

**Example:**

あの先生の友達の本も五百円ですか。= Is that teacher's friend's book also 500 yen?

- あの先生の友達の本: Complete noun phrase
- も: Adds "also" to the noun phrase
- 五百円です: Price predicate
- か: Question particle

The head noun is 本.
The の connections identify which book is being discussed.

## 6. Important Concepts

- いくら means "how much".
- It commonly asks about prices.
- いくらですか asks for a price using a familiar predicate structure.
- 円（えん）is the Japanese yen.
- A price can be given with 円です.
- ではありません can negate a price statement.
- Question words remain in their grammatical positions.
- Kanji spellings for prices are introduced for recognition, not memorization.
