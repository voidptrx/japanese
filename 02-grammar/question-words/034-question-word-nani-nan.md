# Lesson 034: Question Word: 何 (なに・なん)

## 1. What I Learned

- 何 means "what".
- 何 has two common readings: なに and なん.
- The reading depends on the grammatical construction.
- 何 can ask about identity, objects, subjects, and noun categories.
- 何 occupies the position of the information being questioned.

## 2. What Is 何?

何 is an interrogative word meaning "what".

| Reading | Common Usage | Example        |
| ------- | ------------ | -------------- |
| なん    | Before です  | 何ですか       |
| なん    | Before の    | 何の本         |
| なに    | Before を    | 何を読みますか |
| なに    | Before が    | 何がありますか |

These are common patterns, not a complete list of pronunciation rules.

Tae Kim discusses the different readings of 何 in Grammar Guide, Section 3.8.4.

## 3. 何ですか - Asking About Identity

<ins>Example:</ins>\
これは何ですか。= What is this?

| Component | Function                      |
| --------- | ----------------------------- |
| これは    | Topic                         |
| 何です    | Predicate asking for identity |
| か        | Question particle             |

Possible answer:

これは本です。= This is a book.

## 4. 何 with particles

### 何を - Questioning the Object

<ins>Example:</ins>\
先生は何を読みますか。= What does the teacher read?

- 先生は: Topic
- 何を: Questioned object
- 読みます: Predicate
- か: Question particle

を marks the direct object.

### 何が - Questioning the Subject

<ins>Example:</ins>\
先生の部屋に何がありますか。= What is in the teacher's room?

- 先生の部屋に: Location of existence
- 何が: Questioned subject
- あります: Predicate
- か: Question particle

が marks the subject whose identity is being questioned.

### 何の — Modifying a Noun

<ins>Example:</ins>\
これは何の本ですか。= What kind of book is this?

何の modifies 本 and asks about its type, subject or relationship.

Compare:

何を勉強しますか。= What do you study?\
何の勉強をしますか。= What kind of studying do you do?

In the first sentence, 何を is the object.
In the second sentence, 何の modifies 勉強, and を marks the complete noun phrase as the object.

## 5. 何 vs. どこ

| Expression         | Meaning             | Questioned Information |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 何を勉強しますか   | What do you study?  | Object                 |
| どこで勉強しますか | Where do you study? | Action location        |

The question word replaces the information being requested.

The particles retain their grammatical functions.

## 6. Important Concepts

- 何 means "what" and is commonly read なに or なん.
- 何ですか is read なんですか.
- 何の is normally read なんの.
- 何を and 何が use the なに reading in the patterns studied here.
- 何を asks about the direct object.
- 何が asks about the grammatical subject.
- 何の modifies a following noun.
- 何 and どこ ask for different kinds of information.
- Question words remain in their grammatical positions within Japanese sentences.
