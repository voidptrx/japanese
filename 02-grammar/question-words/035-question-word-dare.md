# Lesson 035 - Question Word: 誰

## 1. What is 誰?

誰 (だれ) means "who" and asks about a person's identity.

Like 何 and どこ, is occupies the grammatical position of the information being questioned.

| Word             | Meaning |
| ---------------- | ------- |
| 誰（だれ）       | Who     |
| 何（なに・なん） | What    |
| どこ             | Where   |

## 2. 誰ですか - Asking About Identity

**Example:**

先生は誰ですか。= Who is the teacher?

- 先生は: Topic
- 誰です: Predicate asking for identity
- か: Question particle

The teacher is the topic, and their identity is being questioned.

## 3. 誰が - Question the Subject

**Example:**

誰が本を読みますか。= Who reads the book?

- 誰が: Questioned subject
- 本を: Object
- 読みます: Predicate
- か: Question particle

が marks the grammatical subject whose identity is unknown.

**Compare:**

誰が先生ですか。= Who is the teacher?\
先生は誰ですか。= Who is the teacher?

The first identifies the subject.
The second makes 先生 the topic and questions its identity.

誰は先生ですか is not the normal neutral way to ask this question.

## 4. 誰の — Whose

誰の modifies a following noun and asks about possession or another relationship.

**Example:**

これは誰の本ですか。= Whose book is this?

- 誰の: Modifies 本
- 誰の本: Complete noun phrase

**Compare:**

先生の本 = The teacher's book\
誰の本 = Whose book?

When the noun is understood from context, it can be omitted:

**Example:**

その本は誰のですか。= Whose is that book?

## 5. Sentence Analysis

**Example:**

あの先生は誰の本をどこで読みますか。= Whose book does that teacher read, and where?

| Component  | Function                      |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- |
| あの先生は | Topic                         |
| 誰の本を   | Questioned object noun phrase |
| どこで     | Questioned action location    |
| 読みます   | Predicate                     |
| か         | Question particle             |

誰の modifies 本, while を marks the complete noun phrase 誰の本 as the object.

## 6. Important Concepts

- 誰（だれ）means "who".
- 誰ですか asks for someone's identity.
- 誰が questions the grammatical subject.
- 誰の modifies a noun and means "whose".
- 誰は is not normally used in neutral subject-identification questions.
- は marks the topic; が marks the subject.
- Question words remain in their grammatical positions.
- The particle determines the grammatical relationship, not the question word alone.
