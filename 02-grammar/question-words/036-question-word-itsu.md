# Lesson 036 - Question Word: いつ

## 1. What Is いつ?

いつ means "when" and asks about the time of an event or action.
It remains in the sentence as the expression representing the unknown time.

**Example:**

先生はいつ本を読みますか。= When does the teacher read books?

- 先生は: Topic
- いつ: Questioned time
- 本を: Object
- 読みます: Predicate
- か: Question particle

## 2. いつ vs. Time + に

Specific clock times generally use に.

**Example:**

八時に本を読みます。= I read a book at eight o'clock.\
いつ本を読みますか。= When do you read a book?

Unlike 八時に, いつ normally does not take に
in basic questions about when an action happens.

## 3. いつ with から and まで

| Expression       | Meaning                  |
| ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| いつ             | When?                    |
| いつから         | Since when? / From when? |
| いつまで         | Until when?              |
| いつからいつまで | From when until when?    |

**Examples:**

いつから勉強しますか。= When do you start studying?\
いつまで勉強しますか。= Until when do you study?\
八時からいつまで本を読みますか。= From eight o'clock until when do you read books?

から marks the starting point.
まで marks the endpoint.

## 4. Combining Question Words

先生はいつどこで本を読みますか。
= When and where does the teacher read books?

- いつ: Questioned time
- どこで: Questioned action location
- 本を: Object

The question words keep their individual grammatical roles.

## 5. Important Distinctions

- いつ asks when an action or event occurs.
- いつから asks for its starting time.
- いつまで asks for its ending time.
- いつ normally does not require temporal に.
- A known specific time, such as 八時, generally takes に.
- Question words remain in their grammatical positions.
