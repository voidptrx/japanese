# Lesson 067 — Noon / Daytime (昼)

## 1. Meaning and Reading

**昼（ひる）** — noon; daytime

- Pronunciation: ひる (hiru)
- Mora count: 2 (ひ・る)
- No long vowels.

昼 refers to midday or a broader part of the daytime, depending on context. It does not necessarily mean exactly 12:00.

## 2. Basic Usage

- 昼、店でパンを買います。 — I buy bread at the shop at noon.
- 昼は暖かいです。 — It is warm during the daytime.
- 昨日の昼、友達と映画を見ました。 — I watched a movie with a friend yesterday afternoon.

昼 does not determine tense. The predicate and context determine past, non-past, and continuing actions.

## 3. Daytime Expressions

| Expression | Reading      | Meaning           |
| ---------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| 昼         | ひる         | noon; daytime     |
| 昨日の昼   | きのうのひる | yesterday at noon |
| 今日の昼   | きょうのひる | today at noon     |
| 明日の昼   | あしたのひる | tomorrow at noon  |

**Examples:**

- 昨日の昼、パンを買った。 — I bought bread yesterday at noon.
- 明日の昼、お茶を飲みます。 — I will drink tea tomorrow at noon.

## 4. Particles

| Form | Function                           |
| ---- | ---------------------------------- |
| 昼、 | Neutral time expression            |
| 昼は | Topic marker; may express contrast |
| 昼の | Modifies a following noun          |
| 昼に | Marks the time of an event         |

Both forms are valid:

- 昼、本を読みます。
- 昼に本を読みます。

Both can mean "I read / will read a book at noon."

に explicitly marks the time, but the basic meaning may remain the same.

Unlike 明日, 昼 can naturally take temporal に.

## 5. Daily Habits

毎日 adds an explicit frequency meaning.

- 昼に本を読みます。 — I read books at noon.
- 毎日、昼に本を読みます。 — I read books every day at noon.
- 毎日、昼に本を読んでいます。 — I have an ongoing habit of reading books at noon.

Both non-past and ～ている can express habits, depending on context.

## 6. Plain and Polite Forms

| Plain                        | Polite                           |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 昼、パンを買う。             | 昼、パンを買います。             |
| 昨日の昼、店に行かなかった。 | 昨日の昼、店に行きませんでした。 |
| 今日の昼、お茶を飲んでいる。 | 今日の昼、お茶を飲んでいます。   |

Maintain the intended speech register and preserve tense, polarity, and aspect when translating.

## 7. Important Concepts

- 昼 means noon or daytime and is read ひる.
- 昼 contains two mora and no long vowels.
- 昼 may refer to a broader daytime period, not exactly 12:00.
- Both 昼、 and 昼に are valid time expressions.
- 昼は marks the daytime as a topic or expresses contrast.
- 毎日、昼に explicitly describes a daily noon routine.
- Relative day expressions such as 明日 normally do not take temporal に.
- Distinguish past and non-past predicates.
- Preserve all time, location, object, and action information when translating.
