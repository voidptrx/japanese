# Lesson 065 — Every Day (毎日)

## 1. Meaning and Reading

**毎日（まいにち）** — every day

- Pronunciation: まいにち (mainichi)
- Mora count: 4 (ま・い・に・ち)
- No long vowels.

## 2. Basic Usage

毎日 expresses frequency and describes actions or situations occurring every day.

- 毎日、日本語を勉強します。 — I study Japanese every day.
- 毎日、学校に行く。 — I go to school every day.
- 毎日、本を読みます。 — I read books every day.

Habitual actions commonly use non-past forms.

## 3. Particles

| Form   | Function                               |
| ------ | -------------------------------------- |
| 毎日、 | Neutral frequency expression           |
| 毎日は | Topic marker; often expresses contrast |
| 毎日の | Modifies a noun: daily / everyday      |

**Examples:**

- 毎日、日本語を勉強します。 — I study Japanese every day.
- 毎日の勉強は面白いです。 — Daily study is interesting.

毎日 normally does not take temporal に.

- Correct: 毎日、本を読みます。
- Incorrect: 毎日に本を読みます。

## 4. Habits and Continuing Actions

Both non-past and ～ている forms can describe habits.

- 毎日、本を読みます。 — I read books every day.
- 毎日、本を読んでいます。 — I read books every day / I have been reading books daily.
- 今、本を読んでいます。 — I am reading a book now.

～ている can express ongoing actions, habitual activities, or resulting states, depending on the verb and context.

## 5. Plain and Polite Forms

| Plain                  | Polite                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 毎日、勉強する。       | 毎日、勉強します。       |
| 毎日、本を読む。       | 毎日、本を読みます。     |
| 毎日、学校に行かない。 | 毎日、学校に行きません。 |

Both registers can express daily habits.

## 6. Habits and Exceptions

A general habit can contrast with a specific day.

- 毎日、本を読んでいるけど、今日は読んでいない。
  — I read books every day, but I am not reading today.

- 毎日、日本語を勉強するけど、昨日は勉強しなかった。
  — I study Japanese every day, but I did not study yesterday.

Remember:

- 読まない — do not read / will not read
- 読まなかった — did not read
- 読んでいる — am reading / regularly read, depending on context

## 7. Important Concepts

- 毎日 means every day and is read まいにち.
- 毎日 is a frequency expression, not a relative day name.
- 毎日 normally does not take temporal に.
- Non-past forms commonly express habits.
- ～ている can also describe ongoing habits.
- Time expressions and verb meanings affect aspect interpretation.
- Preserve tense, polarity, and conjunction meaning when translating.
