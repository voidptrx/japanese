# Lesson 066 — Morning (朝)

## 1. Meaning and Reading

**朝（あさ）** — morning

- Pronunciation: あさ (asa)
- Mora count: 2 (あ・さ)
- No long vowels.

## 2. Basic Usage

朝 refers to the morning part of the day.

- 朝、本を読みます。 — I read books in the morning.
- 朝は寒いです。 — It is cold in the morning.
- 朝、学校に行きました。 — I went to school in the morning.

朝 does not determine tense. The predicate and context determine whether an event is past, present, or future.

## 3. Morning Expressions

| Expression | Reading      | Meaning           |
| ---------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| 朝         | あさ         | morning           |
| 今朝       | けさ         | this morning      |
| 昨日の朝   | きのうのあさ | yesterday morning |
| 明日の朝   | あしたのあさ | tomorrow morning  |
| 毎朝       | まいあさ     | every morning     |

**Examples:**

- 今朝は寒くなかったです。 — It was not cold this morning.
- 昨日の朝、本を読みました。 — I read a book yesterday morning.
- 明日の朝、学校に行きます。 — I will go to school tomorrow morning.
- 毎朝、日本語を勉強します。 — I study Japanese every morning.

## 4. Particles

| Form | Function                           |
| ---- | ---------------------------------- |
| 朝、 | Neutral time expression            |
| 朝は | Topic marker; may express contrast |
| 朝の | Modifies a following noun          |

**Examples:**

- 朝は静かです。 — Mornings are quiet.
- 朝の勉強は面白いです。 — Morning study is interesting.

朝 commonly appears without に when indicating time.

- 朝、日本語を勉強します。 — I study Japanese in the morning.

Unlike relative day expressions such as 今日, 朝 can also take に in certain contexts. Do not treat 朝に as universally incorrect.

## 5. Morning and Daily Habits

朝 indicates a part of the day, while 毎朝 explicitly indicates repetition every morning.

- 朝、本を読みます。 — I read books in the morning.
- 毎朝、本を読みます。 — I read books every morning.
- 毎朝、本を読んでいます。 — I regularly read books every morning.

Both non-past and ～ている can describe habits.

## 6. Plain and Polite Forms

| Plain                        | Polite                         |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 朝、学校に行く。             | 朝、学校に行きます。           |
| 今朝、本を読まなかった。     | 今朝、本を読みませんでした。   |
| 毎朝、日本語を勉強している。 | 毎朝、日本語を勉強しています。 |

**Examples:**

- 昨日の朝は寒かったけど、今朝は暑くない。
  — It was cold yesterday morning, but it is not hot this morning.

- 今朝は学校に行きませんでしたが、今は家で本を読んでいます。
  — I did not go to school this morning, but I am reading a book at home now.

Maintain a consistent speech register within the intended sentence or conversation.

## 7. Important Concepts

- 朝 means morning and is read あさ.
- 朝 contains two mora and no long vowels.
- 今朝 means this morning and is read けさ.
- 毎朝 means every morning and is read まいあさ.
- 毎朝 explicitly expresses a repeated morning habit.
- 朝 does not determine the predicate's tense.
- 朝 usually does not require に, but 朝に can be valid in context.
- Non-past and ～ている can both express habits.
- Preserve tense, polarity, aspect, and speech register when translating.
