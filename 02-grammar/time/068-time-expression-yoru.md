# Lesson 068 — Evening / Night (夜)

## 1. Meaning and Reading

**夜（よる）** — evening; night

- Pronunciation: よる (yoru)
- Mora count: 2 (よ・る)
- No long vowels.

夜 refers to the evening or nighttime period.

## 2. Basic Usage

- 夜、映画を見ます。 — I watch movies in the evening.
- 夜は静かです。 — It is quiet at night.
- 昨日の夜、友達に会った。 — I met a friend last night.
- 明日の夜、駅で友達を待ちます。 — I will wait for a friend at the station tomorrow evening.

夜 does not determine tense. The predicate and context determine whether an event is past, non-past, or continuing.

## 3. Evening and Night Expressions

| Expression | Reading      | Meaning                |
| ---------- | ------------ | ---------------------- |
| 夜         | よる         | evening; night         |
| 昨日の夜   | きのうのよる | last night             |
| 今日の夜   | きょうのよる | tonight                |
| 明日の夜   | あしたのよる | tomorrow evening/night |
| 今夜       | こんや       | tonight                |
| 毎晩       | まいばん     | every evening/night    |

**Examples:**

- 今夜、家で料理をします。 — I will cook at home tonight.
- 毎晩、お茶を飲んでいます。 — I drink tea every evening.
- 昨日の夜、映画を見なかった。 — I did not watch a movie last night.

今夜 is read こんや, not いまよる.

## 4. Particles

| Form | Function                           |
| ---- | ---------------------------------- |
| 夜、 | Neutral time expression            |
| 夜は | Topic marker; may express contrast |
| 夜の | Modifies a following noun          |
| 夜に | Marks the time of an event         |

Both forms are valid:

- 夜、映画を見ます。
- 夜に映画を見ます。

毎晩 normally does not require temporal に when expressing frequency.

## 5. Habits and Exceptions

毎晩 explicitly indicates a repeated evening habit.

- 毎晩、お茶を飲む。 — I drink tea every evening.
- 毎晩、お茶を飲んでいる。 — I regularly drink tea every evening.
- 毎晩、お茶を飲むけど、今夜はコーヒーを飲む。 — I drink tea every evening, but I will drink coffee tonight.

Non-past forms and ～ている can both describe habits, depending on context.

## 6. Plain and Polite Forms

| Plain                          | Polite                             |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| 夜、映画を見る。               | 夜、映画を見ます。                 |
| 昨日の夜、お茶を飲まなかった。 | 昨日の夜、お茶を飲みませんでした。 |
| 今夜、料理をしない。           | 今夜、料理をしません。             |

**Examples:**

- 昨日の夜、店でかばんを買いました。 — I bought a bag at the shop last night.
- 今夜、友達と会って、ご飯を食べます。 — I will meet a friend and eat a meal tonight.

With 会う, に and と can both mark the person met, with different nuances.

## 7. Important Concepts

- 夜 means evening or night and is read よる.
- 夜 has two mora and no long vowels.
- 今夜（こんや） means tonight and has three mora.
- 毎晩（まいばん） means every evening/night and has four mora.
- 夜に is a valid temporal expression.
- 毎晩 normally does not take temporal に.
- Non-past forms can express future events and habits.
- ～ている may express continuing actions or habits.
- Specific clock times generally take に.
- まだ読んでいない means "have not read yet."
- Preserve tense, polarity, aspect, and speech register when translating.
