# Lesson 062 — Today (今日)

## 1. Meaning and Reading

**今日（きょう）** — today

- Pronunciation: きょう (kyō)
- Mora count: 2 (きょ + う)
- The final う lengthens the vowel.

## 2. Basic Usage

今日 is a relative time expression referring to the current day.

- 今日、学校に行きます。 — I will go to school today.
- 今日、家で本を読みました。 — I read a book at home today.
- 今日、学校に行きませんでした。 — I did not go to school today.

今日 does not determine tense. The predicate determines whether the action is past or non-past.

## 3. Particles

| Form   | Function                           |
| ------ | ---------------------------------- |
| 今日、 | Neutral time expression            |
| 今日は | Topic marker; may express contrast |
| 今日の | Modifies a noun: today's           |

**Examples:**

- 今日は寒いです。 — It is cold today.
- 今日は学校に行きません。 — I will not go to school today.
- 今日の本は面白くないです。 — Today's book is not interesting.

は does not always imply contrast.

## 4. Time Expressions and に

Relative day expressions such as 今日 generally do not take に when indicating when an event occurs.

- Correct: 今日、学校に行きます。
- Incorrect: 今日に学校に行きます。

Specific dates and clock times may require に.

## 5. Tense and Aspect

| Predicate          | Meaning                 |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- |
| 勉強します         | study / will study      |
| 勉強しました       | studied                 |
| 勉強しません       | do not / will not study |
| 勉強しませんでした | did not study           |
| 勉強しています     | am studying             |

**Examples:**

- 今日、家で日本語を勉強します。 — I will study Japanese at home today.
- 今日、家で日本語を勉強しています。 — I am studying Japanese at home today.
- 今日、家で日本語を勉強しました。 — I studied Japanese at home today.

## 6. Important Concepts

- 今日 means today and is read きょう.
- 今日 has two mora.
- Relative day expressions normally do not require に.
- 今日は marks today as the topic.
- 今日の modifies a following noun.
- Past, non-past, and continuing actions are distinguished by the predicate.
- Preserve tense, polarity, and aspect when translating.
