# Lesson 063 — Yesterday (昨日)

## 1. Meaning and Reading

**昨日（きのう）** — yesterday

- Pronunciation: きのう (kinō)
- Mora count: 3 (き・の・う)
- The final う lengthens the preceding vowel.

## 2. Basic Usage

昨日 refers to the day before today.

- 昨日、学校に行きました。 — I went to school yesterday.
- 昨日、本を読みませんでした。 — I did not read a book yesterday.
- 昨日は寒かったです。 — It was cold yesterday.

昨日 indicates a past day, but the predicate must still be conjugated appropriately.

## 3. Particles

| Form   | Function                           |
| ------ | ---------------------------------- |
| 昨日、 | Neutral time expression            |
| 昨日は | Topic marker; may express contrast |
| 昨日の | Modifies a noun: yesterday's       |

**Examples:**

- 昨日は学校に行きませんでした。 — I did not go to school yesterday.
- 昨日の本は面白かったです。 — Yesterday's book was interesting.
- 昨日は寒かったですが、今日は暑いです。 — It was cold yesterday, but it is hot today.

は does not necessarily imply contrast.

## 4. Time Expressions and に

Relative day expressions such as 昨日 normally do not take に when indicating when an event occurred.

- Correct: 昨日、先生と話しました。
- Incorrect: 昨日に先生と話しました。

This follows the same basic rule as 今日.

## 5. Past Tense and Polarity

| Form                       | Example              | Meaning       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------- |
| Past positive              | 行きました           | went          |
| Past negative              | 行きませんでした     | did not go    |
| い-adjective past          | 寒かったです         | was cold      |
| な-adjective past          | 静かでした           | was quiet     |
| な-adjective past negative | 静かじゃなかったです | was not quiet |

**Examples:**

- 昨日、友達と公園で遊びました。 — I played in the park with a friend yesterday.
- 昨日、この部屋はきれいでした。 — This room was clean yesterday.
- 昨日の本は面白くなかったです。 — Yesterday's book was not interesting.

## 6. Important Concepts

- 昨日 means yesterday and is read きのう.
- 昨日 contains three mora.
- 昨日 normally does not require に as a time expression.
- 昨日は marks yesterday as the topic.
- 昨日の modifies a following noun.
- Use past predicates for completed events and past states.
- Preserve tense, polarity, and aspect when translating.
- Distinguish い-adjective and な-adjective conjugations.
