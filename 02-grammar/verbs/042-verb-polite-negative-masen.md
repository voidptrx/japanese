# Lesson 042 — Polite Negative Verb Form (～ません)

## 1. Polite Negative Non-Past

The ～ません form expresses polite, non-past negation.

| Affirmative | Negative   |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| 読みます    | 読みません |
| 書きます    | 書きません |
| 食べます    | 食べません |
| 来ます      | 来ません   |

Like ～ます, ～ません is non-past. It can refer to present habits or future situations depending on context.

## 2. Conjugation by Verb Group

### Godan (五段動詞)

Change the final kana to its い-row equivalent and add ません.

- 読む => 読みません
- 書く => 書きません
- 行く => 行きません

### Ichidan (一段動詞)

Remove the final る and add ません.

- 食べる => 食べません
- 見る => 見ません

### Irregular Verbs

- する => しません
- 来る（くる）=> 来ません（きません）

## 3. Shared ます-Stem

Both polite affirmative and negative forms use the same stem.

読む => 読み

- 読み + ます => 読みます
- 読み + ません => 読みません

The stem remains unchanged when switching between these two forms.

## 4. Verb vs. Noun Negation

Noun predicates and verbs use different negative constructions.

- 先生ではありません。= Is not a teacher.
- 本を読みません。= Does not read a book.

Do not replace a verb's ～ません with ではありません.

## 5. Example Sentences

先生は学校で本を読みません。= The teacher does not read books at school.

この人は学校で勉強しません。= This person does not study at school.

あの先生の友達は学校に来ません。= That teacher's friend does not come to school.

どの先生が学校で食べませんか。= Which teacher does not eat at school?

## Important Concepts

- ～ません is polite, negative, and non-past.
- Use the verb's ます-stem.
- Verb groups determine stem formation.
- Particles retain their usual roles in negative sentences.
- ～ませんか can also express an invitation or suggestion, depending on context.
