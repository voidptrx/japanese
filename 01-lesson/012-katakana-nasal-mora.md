# Lesson 012 - Katakana Nasal Mora (ン)

## 1. What I learned

- I learned why `ン` is different from most other katakana.
- I understand that `ン` counts as one mora.
- I learned that the pronunciation of `ン` can change depending on surrounding sounds.
- I learned how to distinguish `ン` + `ヤ` from yoon combinations.
- I can distinguish `ソ` from `ン`.
- I can read words containing `ン` and count their mora correctly.

## 2. What is the Nasal Mora?

The katakana `ン` represents a nasal mora.

It is the katakana equivalent of hiragana `ん`.

Unlike most basic katakana, `ン` does not contain a vowel.

However, it still counts as one mora.

Examples:

- パ = pa = 1 mora
- パン = pan = 2 mora
- ペン = pen = 2 mora
- コンビニ = konbini = 4 mora

## 3. Pronunciation Changes

The written character `ン` stays the same, but its
pronunciation can change depending on the following sound.

### Before p, b, and m

`ン` often sounds similar to m.

Example:

- コンビニ = konbini

### Before k and g

`ン` often sounds similar to the final ng sound
in the English word "sing".

Example:

- インク = inku

### At the End of a Word

`ン` can have a different nasal pronunciation
depending on context.

Example:

- パン = pan

Important:

These pronunciation differences do not change
the katakana spelling or mora count.

## 4. ン and Yoon

The following are different:

- ニャ = nya = 1 mora
- ニヤ = niya = 2 mora
- ンヤ = n'ya = 2 mora

A small `ャ` combines with the preceding kana.

The character `ン` remains a separate mora.

Example (katakana reading practice):

コンヤ = kon'ya = 3 mora

- コ = 1 mora
- ン = 1 mora
- ヤ = 1 mora

The word "tonight" is normally written as
こんや or 今夜 rather than コンヤ.

In Hepburn romaji, an apostrophe can distinguish
`ン` from a following vowel or y-sound.

## 5. Mora Counting

| Katakana     | Romaji     | Mora |
| ------------ | ---------- | ---- |
| パン         | pan        | 2    |
| ペン         | pen        | 2    |
| サイン       | sain       | 3    |
| コンビニ     | konbini    | 4    |
| シンカンセン | shinkansen | 6    |
| キャンプ     | kyanpu     | 3    |
| マンション   | manshon    | 4    |
| パンフレット | panfuretto | 6    |

## 6. Important Distinctions

- `ソ` = so, while `ン` = n.
- Small `ャ` / `ュ` / `ョ` combines with the previous kana.
- Small `ッ` counts as one mora.
- `ン` counts as one mora.
- The pronunciation of `ン` may change, but its spelling remains the same.
- `ン` followed by `ヤ` is not automatically a yoon combination.
- Hiragana `ん` and katakana `ン` follow the same pronunciation and mora rules.
