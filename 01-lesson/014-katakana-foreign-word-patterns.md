# Lesson 014 - Katakana Foreign-Word Patterns

## 1. What I learned

- I learned how katakana represents sounds that are not part of the traditional kana table.
- I learned how small vowels (ァ, ィ, ゥ, ェ, ォ) combine with other katakana.
- I understand the difference between small and full-sized vowels.
- I can read common foreign-word patterns.
- I can count mora in words containing these combinations.

## 2. Small Vowels

Katakana can use small vowels to represent additional sounds,
especially in foreign loanwords.

| ァ  | ィ  | ゥ  | ェ  | ォ  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a   | i   | u   | e   | o   |

Unlike full-sized vowels, these small characters can combine
with the preceding katakana to form one mora.

Examples:

- ファ = fa = 1 mora
- フア = fua = 2 mora

- ティ = ti = 1 mora
- テイ = tei = 2 mora

## 3. Common Foreign-Word Combinations

### F Sounds

| ファ | フィ | フェ | フォ |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| fa   | fi   | fe   | fo   |

### T and D Sounds

| ティ | ディ | トゥ | ドゥ |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| ti   | di   | tu   | du   |

Important distinctions:

- チ = chi, but ティ = ti
- ツ = tsu, but トゥ = tu
- デ = de, but ディ = di

### W Sounds

| ウィ | ウェ | ウォ |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| wi   | we   | wo   |

`ウォ` is different from `ヲ`, which is normally pronounced `o`
in modern Japanese.

### SH, J and CH Sounds

| シェ | ジェ | チェ |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| she  | je   | che  |

Compare:

- シャ = sha, シェ = she
- ジャ = ja, ジェ = je
- チャ = cha, チェ = che

## 4. Small vs. Full-Sized Kana

Small vowels change how the characters combine.

- シェ = she = 1 mora
- シエ = shie = 2 mora

- フォ = fo = 1 mora
- フオ = fuo = 2 mora

- ティ = ti = 1 mora
- テイ = tei = 2 mora

The size of the vowel character affects pronunciation
and mora counting.

## 5. Mora Counting

Foreign-word combinations can appear together with
yoon, sokuon, nasal mora and long vowels.

Examples:

ファイル = fairu = 3 mora

- ファ = 1 mora
- イ = 1 mora
- ル = 1 mora

パーティー = paatii = 4 mora

- パ = 1 mora
- ー = 1 mora
- ティ = 1 mora
- ー = 1 mora

ファッション = fasshon = 4 mora

- ファ = 1 mora
- ッ = 1 mora
- ショ = 1 mora
- ン = 1 mora

## 6. Important Concepts

- Small vowels allow katakana to represent additional foreign sounds.
- A combination with a small vowel normally counts as one mora.
- Full-sized vowels are pronounced separately.
- Small ャ / ュ / ョ forms traditional yoon combinations.
- Small ッ adds one mora.
- ン counts as one mora.
- The long vowel mark ー adds one mora.
- Japanese loanword pronunciation does not always match the original language exactly.
