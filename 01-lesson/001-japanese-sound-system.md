# Lesson 001 - Japanese Sound System

## 1. What I learned

- I understand the basic Japanese sound system and how it differs from my main language.
- I understand the difference between kana, mora, and syllable.
- I learned my first 10 hiragana.
- I can read some real Japanese words using my first 10 hiragana.

## 2. Japanese vowels

Japanese has five basic vowels:

- あ = a
- い = i
- う = u
- え = e
- お = o

## 3. First 10 Hiragana

- あ い う え お - a i u e o
- か き く け こ - ka ki ku ke ko

## 4. Important concepts

- Mora: A unit of timing used to measure Japanese rhythm.
- Romaji: Japanese written using the Latin alphabet.
- Kana: Hiragana and Katakana.
- Kanji: A separate Japanese writing system based on Chinese characters.

## 5. First Japanese words

- いえ = home / house
- かお = face
- あお = blue
- ここ = here / this place
- きく = to listen / to hear; to ask
- かき = writing / oyster
