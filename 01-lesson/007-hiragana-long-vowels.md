## 2. What are Long Vowels?

Japanese distinguishes short and long vowels.  
A long vowel takes twice as much time as a short vowel.

Example:

- おばさん = obasan = 4 mora = aunt / middle-aged woman
- おばあさん = obaasan = 5 mora = grandmother
- おじさん = ojisan = 4 mora = uncle / middle-aged meaning
- おじいさん = ojiisan = 5 mora = grandfather

Vowel length is part of a word's pronunciation and can change its meaning.
