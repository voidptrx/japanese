# Lesson 002 - Hiragana Foundations

## 1. What I learned

- Hiragana is organized around a regular sound pattern.
- Japanese basic vowels follow the order `a-i-u-e-o`.
- Many hiragana characters combine a consonant sound with a vowel sound.
- The `か` row keeps the `k` sound while changing the vowel.

## 2. Basic vowel row

| あ  | い  | う  | え  | お  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
|  a  |  i  |  u  |  e  |  o  |

## 3. The `か` row

| か  | き  | く  | け  | こ  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| ka  | ki  | ku  | ke  | ko  |

The consonant stays `k` while the vowel changes:

- か = k + a
- き = k + i
- く = k + u
- け = k + e
- こ = k + o

## 4. Mora

Each hiragana character normally represents one mora.

For example:  
かお = か | お = 2 mora  
きく = き | く = 2 mora

## 5. Important pattern

The basic vowel order is:  
あ い う え お

This pattern is used throughout the hiragana table.  
The `か` row follows the same vowel order:  
か き く け こ  
ka ki ku ke ko

## 6. The `さ` row

| さ  | し  | す  | せ  | そ  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| sa  | shi | su  | se  | so  |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> `し` is pronounced `shi`, not `si`.

## 7. The `た` row

| た  | ち  | つ  | て  | と  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| ta  | chi | tsu | te  | to  |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> `ち` is pronounced `chi`, not `ti`.  
> `つ` is pronounced `tsu`, not `tu`.

## 8. The `な` row

| な  | に  | ぬ  | ね  | の  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| na  | ni  | nu  | ne  | no  |

## 9. The `は` row

| は  | ひ  | ふ  | へ  | ほ  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| ha  | hi  | hu  | he  | ho  |

`ふ` is pronounced `fu`.

For now, `は` is learned as `ha`. Its pronunciation as `wa` when used as a particle will be learned later.

## 10. The `ま` row

| ま  | み  | む  | め  | も  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| ma  | mi  | mu  | me  | mo  |

## 11. The `や` row

| や  | ー  | ゆ  | ー  | よ  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| ya  | ー  | yu  | ー  | yo  |

The `や` row has only three basic characters.  
There is no `yi` or `ye`.

## 12. The `わ` row

| わ  | ー  | ー  | ー  | を  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| wa  | ー  | ー  | ー  |  o  |

`を` is usually pronounced `o` when used as a particle.  
Its grammatical use will be learned later when we study particles.

## 13. `ん` character

`ん` is differnet from most basic hiragana because it represents a consonant sound.  
It normally represents one mora.

For example:
ほん = ほ | ん = 2 mora

## 14. The `ら` row

| ら  | り  | る  | れ  | ろ  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| ra  | ri  | ru  | re  | ro  |
